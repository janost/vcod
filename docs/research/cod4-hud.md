# CoD4 1.7: the 2D layer of a watched session

What vcod draws over a watched CoD4 session and where each piece comes from: fonts and HUD images, the playerstate's hudelems, the server commands the cgame reads (chat, game messages, scores, configstrings), the obituary's kill line and centre print, the compass. Code: `crates/cod4/src/hud.rs` (renderer-free) and `crates/client/src/cod4_hud.rs`. Gates: `crates/cod4/tests/hud_assets.rs` and the client's `cod4_hud` tests.

## Sources

- `iw3mp.exe` 1.7 (image base 0x400000, md5 8c40cde102a9d8297a8674cb149f96ed), read with `objdump -d -M intel`.
- The stock zones `code_post_gfx_mp`, `localized_code_post_gfx_mp`, `common_mp`, `localized_common_mp` and `mp_crash`, walked by `vcod_cod4::walk`.
- The kill and playing capture pairs, `crates/cod4/tests/fixtures/net/play-1.7-*.txt` (cod4-netfields.md 8, 10).
- An INFERRED claim that names no 1.7 address is not yet confirmed on 1.7.

## 2. Server commands

- **Dispatch.** `CG_DeployServerCommand` is `iw3mp.exe` 0x44be40, a byte table at 0x44c5b8 indexing a jump table at 0x44c530. VERIFIED. It switches on the first character of `Cmd_Argv(0)` and sends anything past `v` (0x76) to the unknown-command arm. INFERRED (`cmp ecx,0x76; ja` at 0x44be71). The cases are `a b c d e f g h i j k n o p q r s t u v` and `B C D E F G H I J K L N`, each to its own code (`e` and `f` share one); every other character goes to the unknown-command arm at 0x44c48c. VERIFIED (both tables read out).
- **What each case does.** INFERRED.

  | Letter | Meaning |
  |---|---|
  | `h`, `i` | chat, team chat |
  | `e`, `f` | game message |
  | `c`, `g` | bold game message |
  | `b` | scores |
  | `d` | configstring |
  | `v` | client dvars |
  | `G`, `H` | team 1's, team 2's score |

- **Player connected.** 1.7's line reaches the client as `<space>"MP_CONNECTED\x15<name>^7"`, first byte 0x20. VERIFIED (every join in the four play captures). Tokenized, its first argument is the quoted message, whose `M` no case takes, so the line never shows. INFERRED. That the space is `iprintln`'s command letter, overwritten by a later `va()`, is INFERRED.
- **Scores.** `CG_ParseScores` is `iw3mp.exe` 0x44a350; the `b` case's code at 0x44beac calls it. VERIFIED. It reads `Cmd_Argv(1)` capped at 0x40 rows, then arguments 2, 3 and 4, then seven a row (base index steps by 7 from 7, reading `base - 2` to `base + 4`), stored at row offsets 0, 4, 8, 0xc, a local, 0x14 and 0x1c. INFERRED (control flow at 0x44a393, 0x44a4a5..0x44a603). Fields: count, team 1 score, team 2 score, score limit; per row client, score, ping, deaths, status icon, kills, assists. INFERRED. A status icon counts when 1..8 (`lea eax,[ebp-1]; cmp eax,7` at 0x44a606) and names configstring `2258 + n` (section 7). INFERRED. The four play captures carry no `b`; the score capture does (section 7). VERIFIED.
- **Dvar mirror.** Names at configstrings 20..147, each value 128 slots on. VERIFIED in the kill capture: `friction` at 36, `5.5` at 164; `g_TeamColor_MyTeam` at 47, `0.6 0.8 0.6 0` at 175; `g_TeamColor_EnemyTeam` at 45, `1 0.45098 0.501961 0` at 173.

## 3. Obituaries, kill lines and the centre print

- **Event.** An obituary is the event entity `EV_OBITUARY` (cod4-netfields.md 10): `otherEntityNum` the victim, `attackerEntityNum` the attacker, `eventParm` the weapon or, with 0x80 set, a means of death. VERIFIED (kill capture: parm 27, `m16_gl_mp`, victim 0, attacker 1).
- **Kill line.** `CG_Obituary` writes `^<c>attacker^7 <icon> ^<c>victim`, `^8` the drawn player's team and `^9` the other's (`^7` without teams). The icon is the weapon's `killIcon` (`WeaponDef` +1304), 1.4 font heights square, twice as wide for `killIconRatio` (+1308) 1 or 2 and half as tall for 2, mirrored when `flipKillIcon` (+1312) is set; a means of death picks a `killicon*` material. INFERRED. The M16's words at +1308 and +1312 read 2 and 1. VERIFIED (zone walk). The zones carry `killicondied`, `killiconmelee`, `killiconheadshot`, `killiconcrush`, `killiconfalling`, `killiconsuicide`, `killiconimpact`. VERIFIED.
- **Centre print.** The drawn player's kill prints `CGAME_YOUKILLED`, his death `CGAME_YOUWEREKILLED`, each with `CGAME_TEAMMATE` for a teammate. INFERRED.
- Neither prints while `ps.deltaTime` is set (the killcam). INFERRED. The kill capture's second `EV_OBITUARY` is the killcam's replay and arrives with `deltaTime` set. VERIFIED (the gate counts one kill line).

## 5. Hudelems

- The 40-field `hudelem_t` (cod4-netfields.md 1) packs `alignOrg` and `alignScreen` as CoD2 does (`alignx << 2 | aligny`, `horzalign << 3 | vertalign`). VERIFIED against the kill capture: the perk icons sit at alignScreen 0x1b (right, bottom), alignOrg 0xa, x -5, and draw against the bottom right in the screenshots.
- `he_type_t` is CoD2's with `MAPNAME` 4 and `GAMETYPE` 5 added, `WAYPOINT` 13. INFERRED. The kill capture's elements are types 1, 6, 9 (the respawn countdown, `0:01.1`) and 13. VERIFIED.
- `font` 0..5 map to `UI_GetFontHandle` enums 0, 4, 5, 6, 2, 3 at base scales 0.25, 0.5, 1/3, 0.25, 0.25, 0.25 times `fontScale`. INFERRED. The team splash (`Marine Force Recon`, `OpFor`) is font 3, the objective font. VERIFIED (capture).
- **Flags.** Bit 1 draws in the foreground pass, bit 2 hides the element while dead. INFERRED. Bit 4 is `hideWhenInMenu`: the prematch's text and timer, which `matchStartTimer` marks `hidewheninmenu`, carry flags 4. VERIFIED (the prematch capture, section 13, and `_globallogic.gsc`). `CG_DrawHudElems` skips such an element while `UI_AnyMenuVisible`. INFERRED. vcod skips it while a script menu is up.
- **Pulse.** A text's `fxBirthTime` / `fxLetterTime` / `fxDecayStartTime` / `fxDecayDuration` (`setPulseFX`) type the text in and fade it. vcod reveals a letter every `fxLetterTime` and fades the whole text over the decay, an approximation of the per-letter decay. INFERRED.
- Text and labels are localized-string configstrings `309 + n`, materials `2002 + n` (cod4-handshake.md 5). VERIFIED (capture: material 1 is `faction_128_usmc`).
- **Label and text merge.** `GetHudElemInfo` calls the merge (`iw3mp.exe` 0x438a00) at 0x438d6f only after testing both first bytes. The merge copies the label up to its first `&&1` (`cmp dl,0x26`, `cmp [..+0x11],dl`, `cmp [..+0x12],0x31` at 0x438a18..0x438a23), skips the three bytes, copies the text, then the label's rest; a label without `&&1` is followed by the text. VERIFIED (the disassembly). The killstreak splash's `MP_KILLSTREAK_N` label over `setValue(3)` reads `3 Kill Streak!`, the promotion's rank label over `RANK_ROMANI` `Private First Class I`. VERIFIED (the zone's strings, `cod4_live_three_kills_*`). vcod: `vcod_cod4::hud::consolidate`.
- **Material scale.** A material's drawn size runs from `fromWidth` / `fromHeight` to `width` / `height` over `scaleTime` after `scaleStartTime`, the from side placed off `fromAlignScreen`. INFERRED. The stock progress bar uses it: `_hud_util::updateBarScale` sets the fill `int(width * frac + 0.5)` wide (at least 1) and `scaleOverTime`s it to the full width over `(1 - frac) / rate`; `_gameobjects` calls `updateBar( curProgress / useTime, 1000 / useTime * useRate )` on the use and proximity bars, the HQ radio's among them. VERIFIED (the rawfiles). Fill and black backing share the image `progress_bar_background`. VERIFIED (the zone's materials). vcod lerps in virtual units with one alignment (`cod4_hud::material_size`), which differs from retail only when one side is full-screen aligned. Trap: drawing `width` alone shows the bar full from the first frame. Gate `cod4_live_progress_bar_fills_over_its_scale_time` (vcod-server's `updateBar( 0, 0.5 )` reads under a quarter at the start and between a quarter and nine tenths at 1.2 s). The look against retail is pending a pixel check.

### 5.1 What vcod-server sends

- `G_UpdateHudElemsToClients` runs after the entity pass and before the end frames. INFERRED (the order). vcod's level does the same (`game::cod4::wire::hud_elems`, `Cod4Level::hud_into`).

### 7.1 Scrolling, the scroll bar and the ping graph

### 12.2 Expressions

- **Precedence.** `s_operatorPrecedence` is at `iw3mp.exe` 0x7245c0, 81 ints: `NOOP` 0x7fffffff, `)` 0, `*` `/` `%` 11, `+` `-` 13, `!` 9, the four comparisons 15, `==` `!=` 16, `&&` `||` 25, `(` 99, `,` 80, `&` 17, `|` 18, `~` 9, the shifts 14, every function call (0x17 on) 5. VERIFIED (the bytes, matched whole).
- **Evaluator** (`iw3mp.exe` 0x53f640). VERIFIED (the instructions). It walks the entries in order. An operand goes on the operand stack as a list of one; `(` (16) goes on the operator stack at once; any other operator first runs the stacked ones the test at 0x53f5d0 lets through, then goes on. That test runs the top operator while its rank is below the incoming one's and is not 5 (a call waits for its `)`), or while it is the same operator and 0x53f5b0 calls it non-associative, which is true of `/`, `%` and `-` only. So a repeated `-` chains left to right and the rest of a rank right to left (`10 - 4 + 3` is 3). Past the last entry it runs every stacked operator through 0x53e860, then fails with "Error: stray operands in expression" (0x6e960c) for more than one list left, with nothing for none, and with "Error: More than one operand in expression result" (0x6e9634) for one list of several. Both stacks hold 60: "Invalid expression - too many operands" (0x6e95ac), "operators are nested too deeply" (0x6e95d4). vcod has no such limits, by choice.
- **Operator runner.** 0x53e860 pops one operator and jumps on it through the table at 0x53f46c (0..0x50). VERIFIED (the instructions).
